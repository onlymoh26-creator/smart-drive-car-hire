CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Rental terms at a glance

Bookings are subject to availability, verification and confirmation by Smart Drive Car Hire. Customers must provide valid identification and a valid driving licence where applicable during the verification process.

The standard payment policy is a 50% reservation deposit after the quotation is approved, with the remaining balance paid before vehicle release unless an alternative is approved in writing.

The hirer must use the vehicle responsibly, follow Kenyan traffic laws and return it at the agreed time and location. Applicable fines, damage and extension charges are handled according to the rental agreement.

The full rental agreement will be provided for review before a booking is finalised. This summary does not replace the signed agreement.

✓

Payment verification

Payments are manually verified by an administrator. A screenshot or customer-submitted claim alone does not confirm payment.
